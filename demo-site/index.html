A few things to know

A demo, with
clear boundaries.

Is this a live AI conversation?+

This public demo uses fixed, synthetic scenarios and scripted replies. No model is called. The full MendCue application has a server-side Meta Muse Spark adapter; live model performance and pilot impact are still unverified.

Can I request an actual repair here?+

No. Phoenix Appliance is a demonstration business. Your inputs stay in this browser tab, and “Create demo brief” does not contact anyone, book a visit, or save a service request to a server.

Does a confirmed model mean a confirmed diagnosis?+

No. Confirming identity checks the label transcription. A technician still needs to verify the appliance, documentation, and cause of failure. The demo’s generic example guidance is not a manufacturer manual.
